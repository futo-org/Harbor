# Child Safety Standards

Last updated November 18th, 2026

These are the standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)
for **Harbor**, published by FUTO Holdings Inc. ("FUTO", "we", "us"). They
apply to the Harbor apps and to the servers run by FUTO, which are marked as
operated by FUTO on the [Servers](./servers.md) page. They add to the
[Content Policy](./content-policy.md) and the
[App Store Content Policy](./content-policy-app-stores.md).

## What is prohibited

Child sexual abuse and exploitation is not allowed on Harbor in any form.
You may not use Harbor to create, post, share, request, or link to:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM): any sexual depiction of a minor,
  including photos, video, drawings, and computer-generated imagery.
- Content that sexualizes minors, whether or not it is explicit.
- Grooming: befriending or contacting a minor to enable sexual abuse or
  exploitation.
- Sexual extortion of a minor, or threats to share intimate imagery of one.
- Trafficking of a minor, or advertising or soliciting a minor for sexual
  purposes.
- Content that promotes, normalizes, or gives instructions for any of the
  above.

There are no exceptions for fiction, humor, or artistic intent.

## How we detect and act on it

- **Scanning.** Images published to our servers are scanned against
  known-material hash databases.
- **Removal.** Matches, and any CSAE content we become aware of through
  reports, are deleted from our servers.
- **Reporting to authorities.** CSAM is reported to the relevant authorities.
- **Enforcement.** We stop accepting content on our servers from identities
  that post CSAE content.

Harbor is built on the Polycentric protocol, and other operators run their
own servers under their own policies. We cannot delete content from servers
we do not run. We can, and do, remove it from ours and keep it out of what
our servers serve and recommend.

## Reporting in the app

Anyone can report a post from its "…" menu by choosing **Child safety**. No
account with FUTO is needed beyond the identity you already use in Harbor.
You can also block any user from their profile. Child safety reports about
content on our servers go to FUTO's moderation service and are reviewed by a
member of the FUTO team.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services
first.

## Compliance with the law

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including the reporting
obligations that apply to FUTO as a company based in the United States, and
we respond to valid requests from law enforcement.

## Child safety contact

Questions about these standards, reports that the in-app form does not cover,
and enquiries from law enforcement or regulators about child safety on Harbor:
[support@futo.org](mailto:support@futo.org).

## Changes

We may update these standards. Changes are published on this page with the
date at the top.
