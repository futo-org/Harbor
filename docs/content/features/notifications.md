# Notifications

The Notifications tab lists activity on your posts and profile:

- New followers
- Replies, reposts, and quotes of your posts
- Reactions, showing the emoji used
- Verification requests someone sent you, and completed verifications you
  requested

On Android and iOS, Harbor also sends push notifications. Tapping one takes
you straight to the post or profile it's about.

:::note
At the moment, only events published to `srv.harbor.social` send push
notifications. If your identity doesn't publish to that server, activity
still shows up in the Notifications tab, but you won't receive pushes for
it. See [Servers](../servers.md) for the server list.
:::
