# Reactions

Harbor uses emoji reactions instead of a single like button. Press the
reaction button on a post (press and hold on mobile, hover on web) to pick
from a row of common emoji, or open the full emoji picker for more.

You get one reaction per post: picking a different emoji replaces your
current one, and picking the same emoji again removes it.

Tap the stacked emoji next to a post to see who reacted, with a tab for
each emoji.
