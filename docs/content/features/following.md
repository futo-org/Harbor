# Following

Follow someone from their profile or from search results, and their posts
appear in your Top and Latest feeds. Following takes effect immediately;
there are no private accounts or follow requests.

A profile shows follower and following counts. Tap either to see the full
lists.

Because your identity is a set of keys rather than a server account, your
follows belong to you. The people you follow can be on entirely different
servers than the ones you publish to.
