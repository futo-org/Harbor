# Profiles

A profile shows an avatar, display name, bio, follower and following
counts, and three tabs: the person's posts, the verifications they have
claimed, and the verifications they have vouched for.

Under the display name you'll see either the person's alias
(`user@domain.com`) or a shortened form of their identity key. An alias is
a memorable handle backed by a domain the person controls; see
[Set Up an Alias](../guides/setting-up-an-alias.md) to get one.

## Editing your profile

On your own profile, Edit profile lets you change your avatar, display
name, bio, and alias. The alias is checked against your domain before it
saves, so set up the domain side first.
