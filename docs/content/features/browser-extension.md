# Browser Extension

Harbor Anywhere is the Harbor browser extension for Chrome, Brave, Edge,
Firefox and Safari. Click its toolbar icon to open Harbor beside the page you
are on. In Safari it opens as a popup.

## Signing in

The extension signs in as the identity you use on
[harbor.social](https://harbor.social).

1. Open harbor.social in a tab and sign in there.
2. Open the extension beside that tab.
3. The tab and the extension each show three emoji. If they match, press
   **Approve** in the tab.

Approving adds a signing key for the extension to your identity. It can post
and react as you, but it cannot add or remove keys. See
[Identity & Devices](./identity.md) for how keys and devices work.

## Posts from the extension

Posts made in the extension are marked **Posted with Harbor Anywhere**.
