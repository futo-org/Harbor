# App Store Content Policy

Last updated October 19th, 2026

This policy applies to Harbor when installed from **Google Play** or the
**Apple App Store**. It adds to the [Content Policy](./content-policy.md) for
the servers run by FUTO, listed on the [Servers](./servers.md) page. Where the
two differ, this policy takes precedence for those installs.

## Why there is a separate policy

Apps distributed through Google Play and the App Store must follow those
stores' rules for user-generated content. Both require that the app prohibits
objectionable content in its policies, that users can report content and block
other users, and that reports are acted on. The rules below exist to meet
those requirements. They apply to content created or viewed through a store
install, not to the network as a whole.

## Prohibited content

When using a store install, you agree not to post or share:

- Sexually explicit content, or sexual content involving minors in any form.
- Hate speech: attacks on people based on race, ethnicity, religion,
  disability, age, nationality, sexual orientation, sex, or gender identity.
- Harassment, bullying, or threats against any person.
- Content that promotes or glorifies violence, terrorism, or self-harm.
- Non-consensual intimate imagery.
- Content that facilitates illegal activity.
- Spam, scams, malware, or impersonation of other people or organizations.

Content in these categories is labelled and hidden from store installs. It is
removed from FUTO's servers only where it also falls under
[What we remove](./content-policy.md#what-we-remove) in the Content Policy.

## Filtering

Posts labelled Hate, Self-Harm, Sexually Suggestive, Sexually Explicit, or
Violence are covered with a warning by default and must be revealed to view.
In Settings, Content Moderation, you can hide them entirely. These settings
apply on your device.

## Reporting and blocking

Report any post from its "…" menu and choose a category. Block any user from
their profile to stop seeing their posts and interactions. We aim to review
reports from store installs within 24 hours and to hide content that violates
this policy from store installs.
